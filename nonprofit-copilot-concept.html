Nonprofit Copilot Feasibility Concept

Concept stage
Assessment not yet delivered; implementation and outcomes not established.

Developed a preliminary assessment mapping five nonprofit workflow needs to Microsoft 365 Copilot capabilities, licensing requirements, limitations, and fallback options. Created an illustrative cost model and identified checks needed before making implementation commitments.

The concept

A nonprofit organization was exploring how AI could support reporting and everyday administrative work. I prepared a feature-fit workbook to organize the requested workflows, potential Microsoft capabilities, dependencies, gaps, and follow-up questions. The organization is unnamed in this public summary.

Five workflow needs

  1. Board reporting: Explore a monthly dashboard and the capacity and licensing needed to support it.
  2. SharePoint workflows: Assess options for automating routine work and identify administrative and data-policy checks.
  3. Outlook support: Explore inbox summaries and scheduling assistance, including where user initiation or confirmation may be needed.
  4. Morning review: Explore a scheduled digest drawing on notes, email, and Teams, with source coverage to be tested.
  5. Notes into tasks: Explore OneNote summaries and task extraction, with task hand-off behavior to be verified.

What I prepared

  • A feature-fit assessment with potential features, licensing and administrative prerequisites, gaps, fallback approaches, and verification actions.
  • An illustrative per-user cost model covering Copilot licensing, a qualifying base subscription where needed, and a separate capacity cost to be quoted.
  • A source register for follow-up research and validation.

The workbook's headcount is a placeholder planning assumption. It is not the organization's confirmed user count. Unpriced capacity and unverified requirements remain open inputs to a complete estimate.

Checks before an implementation commitment

  • Confirm the actual users, existing subscriptions, budget, and desired outcomes with the organization.
  • Validate current capabilities and license requirements against official Microsoft documentation and the organization's tenant.
  • Confirm preview availability, administrative settings, permissions, and data-governance requirements.
  • Obtain any additional capacity quote and complete the cost estimate before treating it as a total.
  • Test representative workflows, source access, scheduling confirmation, and task hand-off in an authorized pilot.

Status and skills demonstrated

This work is at the concept stage and has not yet been delivered as a completed client assessment. No rollout, adoption, time savings, or other business results are claimed.

Skills: Requirements analysis, AI feasibility planning, feature-fit assessment, licensing research, cost estimation, and documentation of assumptions and verification needs.
